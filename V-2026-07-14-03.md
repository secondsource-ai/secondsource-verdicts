## 判斷(繁中)

GPU/ASIC 並存分化:訓練留 GPU、hyperscaler 自用推理走 ASIC

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

2027H1 盤點:frontier 訓練 run GPU 佔比仍 >80% 且 hyperscaler ASIC 推理佔比續升=hit

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

任一 ASIC 拿下第三方 frontier 訓練大單;或 ASIC 推理佔比連兩年停滯

## The call (English)

GPUs and ASICs diverge rather than displace each other: training stays on GPUs, while hyperscalers move their own inference to ASICs.

## How it gets settled (English)

Reviewed in H1 2027: if GPUs still hold more than 80% of frontier training runs and the ASIC share of hyperscaler inference keeps rising, the call stands.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

Any ASIC winning a major third-party frontier training contract, or the ASIC share of inference flat for two consecutive years.
