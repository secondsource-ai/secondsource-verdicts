## The call (English)

In robotics, VLA versus world models is unsettled. Cosmos Policy suggests world-model foundations are starting to take ground from VLA.

## How it gets settled (English)

Over 12 months: two or more independent teams reproducing a video or world model post-trained directly into a policy that beats a VLA on the same data confirms the world-model side. No reproduction leaves VLA's default position intact.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

Two or more independent reproductions void VLA-specific architectures as the default. No one reproducing world-model policies downgrades the claim that the boundary is blurring.
