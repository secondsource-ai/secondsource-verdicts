## The call (English)

The most direct test of Bill Gurley's claim that no one dares to raise prices — that Anthropic, afraid of losing market share, will not touch its price: Anthropic's official pricing page says the Claude Sonnet 5 promotional price ends on 2026-08-31. Does the price go back to the standard rate on September 1, a 50% increase?

## How it gets settled (English)

After 2026-09-01, check Anthropic's official pricing page (platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) directly. If Sonnet 5 shows the standard price, the terms were carried out and this issue's call that Gurley's claim does not hold gets its strongest piece of evidence. If the promotion is extended, or the standard price itself is cut, the terms were not carried out.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

If the terms are not carried out (the promotion is extended or the standard price is cut), the first-hand evidence that labs dare to raise prices is void and this issue's call has to be reopened as contested. If, in the same period, the flagship tier also drops by 50% or more and no new, pricier tier appears, the opposite argument — that prices are being quietly watered down — gains support instead.
