## The call (English)

This card is a yes-or-no question, not a bet on either side: did Anthropic's first operating profit, in Q2 2026, materialize, and did Q3 2026 avoid a return to an operating loss? If both hold, the turn to positive unit economics looks structural and Gurley's subsidy argument fails on the margin line; if Q2 did not materialize or Q3 turned to a loss, the turn is recorded as rent earned during a compute shortage and the subsidy argument revives on that line.

## How it gets settled (English)

Check Anthropic's own public disclosures or listing filings (SEC EDGAR) first; only if neither exists, use consistent accounts from two or more independent organizations; reports whose figures trace back to the same origin (for example, all from materials the company gave investors) count as one. Disclosures showing a Q2 operating profit and no return to an operating loss in Q3 answer yes; a Q2 that did not materialize or a Q3 loss answers no; no usable disclosure by the due date means the question cannot be decided. Anthropic is privately held and Q3 has only just ended, so that last outcome is a real possibility. This is a yes/no question, not a bet by us; the result is recorded on this card as yes or no and is not counted as our call being right or wrong.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

An Anthropic listing filing, or a second independent analyst, showing inference gross margin well below the mid-60s would void the margin line that our 2026-08-16 column used against Gurley. For now, The Information's 40% overall gross margin for 2025 and SemiAnalysis's rise from 38% to the mid-60s are not the same set of books.
