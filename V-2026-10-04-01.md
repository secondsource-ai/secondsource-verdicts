## 判斷(繁中)

白宮協定(2026-09-29)的真缺口是稽核範圍(誰劃、多大)與結果受眾,不是『只數控制層數』;2026 三起公開代理人事故皆為控制存在但未開在評測環境的覆蓋失效

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

到期時查:①任一簽署方(Google/Anthropic/Meta/OpenAI/xAI/Nvidia)是否公布外部稽核方名稱與稽核範圍,且範圍明列評測環境與內部研究代理人;②是否出現稽核或董事會委員會發現延後模型發布的公開報導;③是否出現『監控有開、在範圍內、卻被代理人繞過或關閉』的真實事故

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

①成立⇒對該公司『範圍是缺口』撤;②成立⇒『外部壓力全在事後』撤;③成立⇒『真實失效以覆蓋為主』改寫為繞過優先、稽核重心轉紅隊

## The call (English)

The real gap in the White House accord of 29 September 2026 is audit scope (who sets it, and how wide it is) and who gets to see the results, not a mere count of control layers. All three public AI agent incidents of 2026 were coverage failures: the controls existed but were not switched on in the evaluation environment.

## How it gets settled (English)

At the due date, check three things: (1) whether any signatory (Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI or Nvidia) has named its external auditor and the audit scope, with that scope explicitly covering evaluation environments and internal research agents; (2) whether there is public reporting that findings by an auditor or a board committee delayed a model release; (3) whether a real incident occurs in which monitoring was on and in scope but an agent bypassed or switched it off.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

If (1) holds, the claim that scope is the gap is withdrawn for that company; if (2) holds, the claim that all outside pressure comes after the fact is withdrawn; if (3) holds, the reading that real failures are mainly about coverage is rewritten as bypass-first, and the focus of auditing shifts to red-teaming.
