## 判斷(繁中)

長文件專業工作上,量身包法相對通用代理的優勢主要來自覆蓋率(把資料讀完),而非專屬程式碼;照預設跑的通用代理讀太少,開啟子代理分派後差距大幅縮小;換對包法後根模型差距仍與包法效果同量級,後訓開源主代理暫未追上前沿模型

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

到期時查:①同類長文件盡職調查任務上,開啟子代理分派(或 dynamic workflows)的 Claude Code/Codex 是否仍落後專屬遞迴包法 ≥20 分;②後訓開源主代理(如 Harvey GLM-5.3)在同包法保留集上是否 ≥ 最好前沿模型(Opus 5 77.6%);③第三方以真實資料室或律師評分重做,覆蓋率與分數的關係是否消失

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

①成立⇒『差距主要是沒讀完』撤,『通用代理是錯的預設』恢復;②成立⇒『後訓開源輸前沿』撤,錨後半部分恢復;③成立⇒覆蓋率機制撤

## The call (English)

In long-document professional work, the edge that purpose-built harnesses hold over general agents comes mainly from coverage, meaning reading all the material, not from proprietary code. General agents run on default settings read too little, and the gap narrows sharply once subagent dispatch is switched on. With the right harness, the gap between root models is still of the same order as the harness effect itself, and post-trained open-model lead agents have not yet caught up with frontier models.

## How it gets settled (English)

At the due date, check: (1) on comparable long-document due-diligence tasks, whether Claude Code or Codex with subagent dispatch (or dynamic workflows) switched on still trails a purpose-built recursive harness by 20 points or more; (2) whether a post-trained open-model lead agent (such as Harvey's GLM-5.3) matches or beats the best frontier model (Opus 5 at 77.6%) on a held-out set under the same harness; (3) whether a third party redoing the test with real data rooms or lawyer grading finds that the link between coverage and score disappears.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

If (1) holds, the claim that the gap is mainly about not reading everything is withdrawn and the claim that general agents are the wrong default is restored; if (2) holds, the claim that post-trained open models lose to frontier models is withdrawn and the second half of the original anchor is restored; if (3) holds, the coverage mechanism is withdrawn.
