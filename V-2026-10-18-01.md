## 怎麼算對(繁中)

CoreWeave Q3 2026 季度財報新聞稿(預計 2026-11 中旬發布)發布後寬限 14 天;以新聞稿寫的季營收×4,除以同一新聞稿寫的季末 active power(GW),算出每 MW 年化收入

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

若該比值 ≥ 每 MW 年化 1,000 萬美元,『溢價集中在邊際、機隊平均短期不跟上』錯,是整體在重定價;本篇第三節與錨 insight 的核心句一起作廢,改讀為整體漲價

## The call (English)

In autumn 2026, tightness in GPU compute shows up as a term premium: short contracts of 3 to 6 months and cancellable large-order contracts run $40 million to $60 million per MW per year, against roughly $10 million to $16 million for five-year contracts. CoreWeave’s fleet-average revenue per MW will not catch up with the marginal price in the near term: Q3 2026 quarterly revenue annualized, divided by end-of-quarter active power, will still be below $10 million per MW per year.

## How it gets settled (English)

Grace period of 14 days after CoreWeave’s Q3 2026 earnings press release (expected mid-November 2026). Take the quarterly revenue stated in the release times 4, divided by the end-of-quarter active power (GW) stated in the same release, to get annualized revenue per MW.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

If the ratio is $10 million per MW per year or more, the reading that the premium sits at the margin and the fleet average does not keep up in the near term is wrong: the whole fleet is being repriced. Section three of this piece and the core sentence of its anchor insight are withdrawn together, and the reading becomes an across-the-board price rise.
