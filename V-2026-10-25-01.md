## 判斷(繁中)

KV 壓縮把記憶體需求從 HBM 容量換到 HBM 速度與 GPU 外記憶體;NVIDIA 下一代 GPU 的 HBM 先加速度、不先加容量。本篇押:NVIDIA 正式公布的 Rubin Ultra 每 GPU HBM 容量不高於 288 GB(Rubin 官方值)。

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

NVIDIA 官方公布 Rubin Ultra 每 GPU HBM 容量(技術部落格、產品頁或 GTC 主題演講投影片);寬限 30 天。

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

Rubin Ultra 每 GPU HBM 容量 >288 GB ⇒ 第三、四節『HBM 往快不往高』要改寫。

## The call (English)

KV compression moves memory demand from HBM capacity to HBM speed and to memory outside the GPU, so NVIDIA's next-generation GPUs will add HBM speed before they add HBM capacity. This piece's bet: the per-GPU HBM capacity NVIDIA officially publishes for Rubin Ultra will be no higher than 288 GB, the official Rubin figure.

## How it gets settled (English)

Settled by NVIDIA's official per-GPU HBM capacity for Rubin Ultra, as published on its technical blog, a product page or GTC keynote slides; 30-day grace period.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

If Rubin Ultra's per-GPU HBM capacity is above 288 GB, sections three and four of the piece, which argue that HBM is getting faster rather than taller, must be rewritten.
