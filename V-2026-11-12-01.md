## 判斷(繁中)

NVIDIA 的兩類賣方信用支持在下一份季報仍照兩種觸發揭露:SB Energy(PORTS)擔保由承租方違約觸發,AI cloud agreements 的承諾隨第三方使用而減少

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

以 SEC EDGAR 上 NVIDIA(CIK 1045810)截至 2026-10-25 這一季的 10-Q 中 Guarantees 與 Additional Commitments 兩段的文字為準;寬限 15 天

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

該 10-Q 把 SB Energy 擔保改寫為不以承租方違約為付款觸發,或把 AI cloud agreements 的承諾改寫為不隨第三方使用而減少

## The call (English)

NVIDIA’s next 10-Q keeps the same two trigger wordings: the SB Energy guarantee is triggered by certain tenant defaults, and the AI cloud agreements commitment decreases as third parties use the capacity.

## How it gets settled (English)

Settled on December 31, 2026 (15-day grace) by the text of the Guarantees and Additional Commitments sections of NVIDIA’s 10-Q for the quarter ended October 25, 2026, on SEC EDGAR. Right if both wordings stand; wrong if either is rewritten to the opposite; void if no such 10-Q is filed or one arrangement is no longer listed.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

The 10-Q rewrites the SB Energy guarantee so payment is no longer triggered by tenant default, or rewrites the AI cloud agreements commitment so it no longer falls as third parties use the capacity.
