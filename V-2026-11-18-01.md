## 判斷(繁中)

## 怎麼算對(繁中)

以 Silicon Data H100 Neo(非三大雲)租價指數在 2027-03-31 當日或之前最近一個公布日的讀數為準;寬限 15 天

## 什麼情況算我們錯(繁中)

## The call (English)

Non-hyperscaler H100 rental prices stay above their Q3 2026 average through March 31, 2027 (Silicon Data H100 index at or above $2.68 per GPU-hour; it was $3.06 on November 10, 2026): older-chip rents have not fallen since the newer generation launched.

## How it gets settled (English)

Settled by the reading of Silicon Data’s H100 index (non-hyperscaler) on March 31, 2027 or the last publication date before it, with a 15-day grace period. At or above $2.68 per GPU-hour, the call is right; below, it is wrong. It is void if Silicon Data stops publishing the index or changes its method so it is no longer comparable with the November 2026 reading. If both outcomes could apply, the call is wrong.

## What would prove us wrong (English)

Silicon Data’s H100 index reads below $2.68 per GPU-hour.
